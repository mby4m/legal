Privacy Policy — Where Am I

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Summary

Where Am I reads your location only while you are looking at the app, shows it on the screen, and then forgets it. Your location is never stored and is never transmitted to the developer. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

Your location

When you open the app or tap Refresh, it asks Android for your current position. The coordinates are shown on screen so you can copy them. They are held in memory only for as long as the screen is open, and are gone when you close the app. Nothing is written to storage and nothing is sent to the developer, who has no server and receives no data of any kind.

How the address is looked up

To turn coordinates into a city, province, country and street, the app asks the geocoding service built into your Android device (android.location.Geocoder). That lookup is performed by the operating system, not by this app, and on most devices it is provided by Google under Google's own privacy policy. The app itself holds no internet permission and makes no network connections. If your device has no geocoding service available, the app simply shows the coordinates.

Copying

Tapping a Copy button places that text on your device's clipboard, at your request. What you then paste it into is up to you; the app has no part in it.

Data stored on your device

One value: whether the location permission prompt has already been shown, so the app can tell "not asked yet" apart from "declined". It contains no location information and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, used only to show you where you are. Granting coarse location alone is supported; the app will simply show a less precise position. It does not request background location, and cannot read your location when it is not on screen. It requests no other permissions.

Children

The app is distributed for users aged 18 and over, and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
