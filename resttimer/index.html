Privacy Policy — Rest Timer

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Summary

Rest Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access of any kind. It cannot send data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app saves one value locally: the countdown length you last selected, so it is preselected next time you open it. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests only VIBRATE, used to buzz when a countdown finishes. It requests no other permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
