Privacy Policy — RPM Boss

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Summary

RPM Boss listens to a running engine and tells you how fast it is turning. The recording is made on your phone, analysed on your phone, and gone when you close the app or record again. No audio is ever stored permanently or sent anywhere. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.

The recording

When you press Record, the app captures up to ten seconds of sound through the microphone. Those samples are held in memory while you crop them and read the result. They are not written to storage, and they are replaced the moment you record again or load a different file. Closing the app discards them.

What the app does with the sound

The analysis runs entirely on your device: it filters the audio, finds the rhythm of the combustions, and turns that into a number. The developer has no server, receives no audio, and receives no results. The app makes no network connections of any kind.

Files you choose

Instead of recording, you can pick an audio file you already have. The app reads the file you select and nothing else. It cannot browse your storage; Android hands it only the one file you chose, and that file is read, not copied or uploaded.

Data stored on your device

Three settings, so the app looks the way you left it: your chosen language, light or dark theme, and whether the optional "expected range" panel is open or closed. They contain no audio and no personal information, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests RECORD_AUDIO, used only while you are recording, and only in the foreground. It does not record in the background and cannot listen when it is not on screen. If you decline, the app still works for audio files you already have. That is the only permission the app requests. In particular it does not hold INTERNET, so it cannot make a network connection of any kind, whatever it might be asked to do.

Children

The app is distributed for users aged 18 and over, and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
