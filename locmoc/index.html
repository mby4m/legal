Privacy Policy - LocMoc

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Summary

LocMoc sets your phone's location to a place or route you choose, so other apps see that location instead of your real one. Your routes and settings stay on your phone. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising, and the developer has no server.

Routes and locations you create

The routes you draw, the single locations you set and the track files you import are stored on your phone only. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your real location

If you allow location access, the app shows your real position as a blue dot on its map, so you can see where you are. It reads your location only while the app is on screen and not faking a location. Your real location is never stored and never sent anywhere.

The location the app sets

While a route or location is running, the app passes positions to Android's mock-location system, so other apps on your phone receive them. This works only after you turn on Developer options and choose LocMoc as the mock location app yourself. It stops when you press Stop. The app keeps a notification visible the whole time it runs.

Map and elevation data

LocMoc collects no data: nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer, who has no server. To draw the map, your phone downloads map tiles directly from OpenFreeMap, based on OpenStreetMap; if you turn on Altitude, it also downloads elevation tiles (Mapzen Terrain Tiles on Amazon Web Services) for the area of your route. Like any map app, these are ordinary web requests for a map area: they carry no route, no account and no device identifier. OpenFreeMap states it keeps no user database and uses no cookies.

Files you choose

You can import GPX, KML or GeoJSON track files. The app reads only the file you pick or share with it, and saves the track inside the app on your phone. It cannot browse your storage.

Data stored on your device

Your saved mocks and a few settings: map style, language, and whether Jitter and Altitude are on. Elevation tiles are cached to avoid downloading them again. All of it stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION show your real position on the map, and are required to run the location service in the foreground. POST_NOTIFICATIONS shows the running notification with its Pause and Stop buttons. FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION keep the location updating while the screen is off. WAKE_LOCK does the same while the phone sleeps. INTERNET downloads map and elevation data, and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE let the map library check whether a connection is available. ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION provides the location you set, and only works after you select LocMoc in Developer options. The app does not request background location.

Children

The app is distributed for users aged 18 and over, and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
